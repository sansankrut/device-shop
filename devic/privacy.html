Limited Warranty Statement

Last Updated: August 10, 2025

WIMOUSE is committed to providing high-quality mice to our customers. This Limited Warranty Statement outlines our warranty terms and conditions. Warranty service is only provided to the original end-user customers only.

Warranty Coverage

WIMOUSE mice are covered by a 3-year limited warranty starting from the date of delivery.

This warranty only covers functional defects caused by manufacturing or material issues.

Cosmetic issues (e.g., scratches, paints wear, minor discolorations) are not covered.

To ensure proper analysis, please keep your order number or purchase confirmation, proof of purchase.

All warranty claims must be submitted via email to support@peripro.com

Please include the following:

  • Your order number
  • A brief description of the issue
  • Photos or videos showing evidence of the issue
