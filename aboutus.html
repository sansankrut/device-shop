ABOUT US

PI PERIPRO is a new name on the
market of computer equipment
and components for it!

PI PERIPRO, founded in 2013, offers the best prices and a constantly expanding selection thanks to constant market monitoring. We strive to stock the most popular and highest-quality computer products.

Over the years, we've earned the trust of both large companies and everyday computer users. We've established ourselves as a reliable and honest supplier of computers and their components. Thanks to this, our young and dynamic team of professionals continues to move forward with greater success and increased motivation!

We are customer-oriented

We offer affordable computers, components, and everything related to them. Based on your preferences and needs, our specialists will select any configuration for your computer. If you plan to build your own computer, our staff will also provide valuable recommendations on suitable accessories, depending on your intended use.

Our sales method is not remote, as you have the opportunity to examine the product in person before purchasing it, when choosing it, and when concluding the purchase agreement in the store's sales area.
